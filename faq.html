Getting started

How fast can I launch a campaign? {{ s0 }}

Under 60 seconds once you're set up. Onboarding — connecting ad accounts, brand kit, and service area — takes about a day, and we do it with you.
Do I need existing ad accounts? {{ s1 }}

Helpful, not required. If you have them we run inside yours so the history and audiences stay with you. If not, we create them in your name during onboarding.
What kinds of businesses use DeepThought? {{ s2 }}

Small and medium businesses running their own paid media — home services, healthcare and dental, legal, fitness, retail, restaurants — plus franchise systems and agencies serving them.

How the engine works

Does Deep Thought manage campaigns after launch? {{ s3 }}

Yes — that's the point. Creative refreshes, bid and budget changes, audience adjustments, and reporting continue for as long as the campaign runs. You set the direction and the guardrails.
Can I edit what it produces? {{ s4 }}

Everything. Headlines, images, audiences, budgets, placements. You can also require approval before anything publishes, or let it run and review afterwards.
Which channels do you support? {{ s5 }}

Every digital channel: search, shopping, and local service ads; social and short-form video; streaming video and connected TV; programmatic display and native; streaming audio; local and marketplace placements; email and SMS; and retail media.
Is a human involved at all? {{ s6 }}

Every account has a named marketing expert for strategy, escalation, and review calls. Deep Thought does the execution; the human is there when judgment beats throughput.

Pricing & commitment

How much does it cost? {{ s7 }}

Pricing is per product and runs about 50% below a comparable agency retainer. Bundling products lowers the per-product rate. See pricing or ask for a quote.
Do you take a percentage of ad spend? {{ s8 }}

No. Your spend goes to the platforms unmarked up, and our fee doesn't grow because you scaled your budget.
Is there a long-term contract? {{ s9 }}

No. Month to month, cancel any time. Your ad accounts, creative, and audience data stay yours if you leave.
What happens to my data? {{ s10 }}

It's yours. We use your campaign and conversion data to optimize your campaigns — not to train models for other advertisers, and never sold.
