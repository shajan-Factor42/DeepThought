DeepThought — paid media execution platform

DeepThought is a paid media execution platform for small and medium businesses. You describe a promotion in plain language; an AI engine builds the creative, the targeting, and the channel mix, publishes the campaign across 30+ channels in under 60 seconds, and keeps optimizing, reallocating budget, rewriting creative, and reporting for as long as the campaign runs. Channels include paid search, paid social, connected TV, and display. Month to month, published pricing, no long-term contract. Nationwide in the United States.

How fast can I launch a campaign?

Under 60 seconds from prompt to live. Connect your ad accounts once; after that every campaign is a single description away.

What kinds of businesses use DeepThought?

Small and medium businesses running their own paid media — services, retail, healthcare, home services — from one location to a few dozen.

Does Deep Thought manage campaigns after launch?

Yes. Optimization, budget reallocation, creative refreshes, and reporting continue for as long as the campaign runs. You approve the direction; it does the work.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month to month, published pricing, cancel any time. Your ad accounts and creative stay yours.

How much does DeepThought cost?

Pricing is per product, month to month. Starting Off — paid search, programmatic display and YouTube Performance Max — is $350 per month. Full funnel, which runs search, social, video and display as one connected plan, is $750 per month. Multi-location and enterprise is quoted custom. Managed-spend products carry a 10–20% management fee and media is billed at cost in your own ad accounts.

Is DeepThought an agency?

No. DeepThought is a platform that performs the execution work an agency would — creative, targeting, channel mix, optimization, reporting — without a retainer or a briefing cycle. A human expert is available when you want one.
