## One connector, any AI assistant

Deep Thought plans and runs digital advertising for small and local businesses across 22 channels, including Google search ads, Local Services Ads, Facebook and Instagram, Nextdoor, YouTube and streaming TV. Our connector links your AI assistant to your Deep Thought account, so you can:

- **Build a media plan** with a channel-by-channel dollar split, first-month steps and the numbers to watch. A copy is emailed to you.
- **Submit it as a campaign.** You see a summary first, and it's sent to our team only after you say yes.
- **Save a payment card** on Stripe's secure page. Nothing is charged until a campaign is approved.
- **Check your campaigns and results**: spend, impressions, clicks, and calls or leads where tracking is set up.
- **Request changes** to budgets, channels or ads, or restart a paused campaign.
- **Pause ads** right away if you need to stop spending.

The connector address is the same everywhere:

`https://mcp.deepthought.marketing/mcp`

## Claude

Full steps, examples and troubleshooting are on our [Deep Thought for Claude](claude-connector.html) page. In short: in Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector**, name it **Deep Thought**, paste the address above and sign in with your Deep Thought account.

## ChatGPT

Deep Thought isn't in ChatGPT's app directory yet. Until it is, you can add it yourself:

1. In ChatGPT on the web, open **Settings**, then **Apps & Connectors**, then **Advanced settings**, and turn on **Developer mode**. On Business, Enterprise or Edu plans, your workspace admin may need to allow it first.
2. Back in **Apps & Connectors**, choose **Create**.
3. Name it **Deep Thought**, paste the address above and choose **OAuth** for sign-in.
4. Sign in with your Deep Thought business account and allow access, then select **Create**.
5. In a new chat, select **+** next to the message box, then **More**, and choose **Deep Thought**.

Which ChatGPT plans can use connectors that take actions (like submitting a campaign) varies by plan and changes over time. Check OpenAI's help pages for **Developer mode** if a step looks different.

## Gemini

1. On [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com), open **Settings**, then **Connected apps**.
2. Under **Custom apps**, choose **Add a custom app**.
3. Paste the address above and select **Next**.
4. Sign in with your Deep Thought business account and allow access.

Once added on the web, Deep Thought also shows up in the Gemini mobile app. Custom apps aren't available for every Google account type yet; if you don't see the option, use Claude or ChatGPT for now.

## Other AI tools

Any AI tool that can add a remote connector (MCP server) by web address can use Deep Thought, including Cursor and VS Code. Add the address above as a remote server, and sign in with your Deep Thought account when asked.

## How review, changes and payment work

| What you ask for | What happens |
|---|---|
| A media plan | Built right away and emailed to you. Nothing is ordered. |
| Submit a campaign | You see a summary. After you say yes, it goes to our team for review, usually within 1 business day. |
| Save a card | You save it on Stripe's secure page. It's charged only after the team approves a campaign. |
| A change | You see a summary. After you say yes, our team reviews it before anything changes. |
| Pause ads | You confirm, and the ads stop right away. Restarting goes through review. |

## Your data

When you sign in, your Deep Thought account shares your name, email address, business name and account ID with the connector so it can work on your business's behalf. What you tell the assistant about your business is used to build your plans and campaigns. Read our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for what we collect, how long we keep it and who we share it with. What you type into the AI assistant itself is covered by that company's privacy policy.
