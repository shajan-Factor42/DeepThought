About us

Good media buying shouldn't be a luxury good

Enterprise brands have teams, tooling, and analysts. Everyone else gets a template, a junior account manager, or a spare hour on Sunday night. DeepThought exists to close that gap — not with more software to learn, but with an engine that does the work.

What we believe

Execution is the bottleneck

Most businesses don't lack ideas about what to promote. They lack the twelve hours a week it takes to build, launch, and maintain the campaigns.

Delegation beats copilots

A tool that suggests still leaves the work with you. Deep Thought is built to be handed a decision and to finish it.

Pricing should be published

Per product, month to month, no percentage of spend. If our fee grows every time you scale, our incentives aren't yours.

Report on revenue, not clicks

Impressions are an input. We tie spend to booked jobs, patients, orders, and sales — the numbers you actually run the business on.
